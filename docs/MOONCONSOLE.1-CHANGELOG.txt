Anya Storage Plus 0.3.0-beta.7-moonconsole.1
Visual test beta for Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249 and Java 21

The Cupid Tablet rests lower in either hand. Its grip, case and glass move
together through the raise, quick turns and return on Escape. The server
controls the appearance chance, which still defaults to 24 percent.

The 216-slot UwU Storage Cube has a larger crescent-marked shell, a hinged lid
and two new chimes. The Anya Storage Hub has a recessed mint monitor. Its
light trails play a fixed loop on each faceplate. Owners keep their existing
storage, labels and automatic links. The 54-slot starter chest and Home Void
Cube keep their distinct designs.

This candidate includes the Home storage guide and the in-game pocket commands.
