Anya Storage Plus 0.3.0-beta.7-tablethandoff.1
Experimental beta for Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249 and Java 21

- The held Cupid Tablet can draw the current animated item texture used
  in the hotbar. An authorized raise carries the visible face through
  the motion before the usual optional intro.
- The wrist and tablet now share the first-person view lag and walking
  motion. That motion eases out as the inventory settles.
- Pausing keeps the same held position and pauses the detailed idle
  timeline. Closing storage restores the natural held position.
- Includes the saved /anya pocket enable, disable and status commands.
