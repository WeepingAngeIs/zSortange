# Edit your Home Void room

In a world where pockets and room editing are enabled, visit through your linked Home Void Cube. Inside the room, right-click the air with the Cupid Tablet linked to that cube. The curved UwU Tablet uses the same room controls in Survival and Creative.

1. Click the wrench marked **Edit Mode · OFF**. The panel closes and the action bar confirms editing is on.
2. Hold a plain supported block in your main hand. Right-click a face to place one block beside it. Left-click a removable decoration to return its item to your inventory. Leave space for the returned item.
3. Right-click an Anya chest to open it with your usual storage permissions. Reopen the linked tablet to see the wrench marked **ON**. Click it to finish editing.
4. Choose **Leave room**, use the exit lodestone, or use `/anya pocket exit` to return. Leaving, logging out or losing access ends edit mode.

**Tooltips: On / Off** stays in the upper-right of the inventory. It controls hover help for your player in this world. The wrench always shows its name and state.

## What you can place

The room accepts stone bricks, quartz blocks, oak planks, cherry planks, black concrete, purple concrete, cyan concrete and sea lanterns. It also supports the Keepsake Globe and Delivery Hatch under their existing interaction rules. Use plain items for building. Renamed or component-bearing items stay in your inventory.

A plain **Labelled Storage Chest** goes on an available marked chest pad and costs one chest, including in Creative. Four starter chests are permanent. The four optional pads allow up to eight registered chests in total, subject to the server’s lower cap. Empty an optional chest before removing it. The room protects its shell, starter chests, landing, chest approaches and exit route.

The exterior picture uses the registered chest positions. An authorized viewer can see the chest arrangement. Everyone else sees the public illustration. Decorations and items inside containers stay private.

## Guest access and server settings

A guest needs **Visit** and **Build** for ordinary room edits. Chest and delivery-hatch edits also require **View**, **Deposit** and **Withdraw**. Opening the tablet keeps the current physical visit and checks the held tablet. The storage actions check their own permissions. Claims can deny a placement or interaction.

Operators can enable pockets with `/anya pocket enable` and enable editing with `/anya storage settings set expansion.pocketEditing true`. The wrench is personal to the current visit. The **Room plan** control and existing build commands remain available. [Full field-kit controls](FIELD_KIT.md)
