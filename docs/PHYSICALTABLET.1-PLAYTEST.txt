Cupid Tablet and Remote playtest for 0.3.0-beta.7-physicaltablet.1

[ ] In a disposable client, find the Cupid Tablet alongside the heart Remote
    in the Anya Storage creative tab. Check the silver model in the hotbar,
    both hands, third person, while paused, and as a dropped item or in a
    frame. Look for the baked blink and glance during the idle loop.
[ ] Use an existing bound Remote. Check that it still opens the same storage
    immediately. Craft the tablet with ordinary materials, crouch-right-click
    the hub to bind it, then use it within hub range.
[ ] Check the tablet's first-person blink and idle. Force the intro chance to
    100%, Esc out during the intro, and verify the tablet is still held.
    At 0%, storage should open without the intro. Restore the previous setting.
[ ] Check that the Home Void Cube reports the disabled pocket gate. With both
    gates enabled in the disposable setup, crouch-right-click the cube with
    either hub-bound item to link it. Uncrouch and right-click the cube to
    visit. From home, verify each item opens the same pocket slots and pad
    count. A guest without View access must see neither.
