Anya Storage Plus 0.3.0-beta.7-pocketplan.2
Experimental test candidate for Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249 and Java 21

- Click Room upgrades in the pocket welcome or the existing chest-pad help to
  open the planner. It shows the four pads and the price before you spend.
- Apply adds one physical chest and 54 slots. It takes one plain Labelled
  Storage Chest after the server rechecks your access and the target.
- The framed preview draws the added chest from the verified room layout.
  The existing local TV cues follow the preview.
- Includes the fieldkit.3 silver Remote and its Remote-only Cupid intro.
  Existing bindings, saved storage, and permissions use their current formats.
