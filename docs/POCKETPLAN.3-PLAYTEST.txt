Anya Storage Plus pocketplan.3 playtest

Setup
[ ] Use the same candidate JAR on a disposable server and both test clients.
    Use Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249 and Java 21. Start from a copy of a
    test save with an already linked pocket, a bound Remote, four starter
    chests and four empty optional pads. Give each player one plain Labelled
    Storage Chest. Start with the guest authorized to Visit only.
[ ] On the test server, use -Danyastorage.pocketPrototype=true. Enable
    pocketStorage and pocketEditing and set pocketContainers to at least 5.
    On both clients use -Danyastorage.chestLayoutStill=true, leave tabletIntro
    at its default and check that Master and Blocks volume are audible.

Tablet, preview and upgrade
[ ] From home, use the existing bound Remote within the hub radius. With
    Tablet opening appearance chance at 100%, check the silver flower tablet,
    two blinks and an inventory click as soon as the controls appear. At 0%,
    verify immediate access. With both cameos off, open an Anya chest and a
    backpack directly and verify immediate access. Restore the prior chance.
[ ] With View access, open the bound pocket Remote. Look for 216 real slots
    and 0/4 upgrade pads occupied, including while a search is active.
    Check that the text fits at your GUI scale and the Upgrades tooltip
    points to the Room upgrades link during a visit. The Visit-only guest,
    the ordinary hub Remote and a direct chest show no pad tally.
[ ] While the owner is inside the pocket, the Visit-only guest looks at the
    Void Cube from home and should see the public fallback. Swap positions.
    With the guest inside to keep the room loaded, the owner sees four
    chests from home. Look steadily, then away, to hear one quiet cue in each
    direction. Looking alone leaves you at home with no access through the
    preview. Right-click to visit and use the lodestone to return.
[ ] On arrival, the owner opens chat and clicks Room upgrades. Check the four
    pad statuses, the price of one plain chest, and the 54-slot gain. Opening
    and marking must leave the held chest count unchanged. Check that a
    second chest mark is refused, then cancel the owner's test plan. The
    Visit-only guest must see no private pad status and keep their chest
    after a denied attempt.
[ ] From the home hub, grant the guest the existing Build, View, Deposit and
    Withdraw rights. During a visit, both players mark the same vacant pad.
    The owner applies first and spends one chest. The guest applies the stale
    plan and keeps their chest. Verify the pad now says Occupied.
[ ] With the guest still inside, return home as the owner. Check that the
    framed preview shows the fifth chest on the chosen pad and the same
    bound Remote shows 270 slots and 1/4 upgrade pads occupied. Stash a
    test item in the added chest. After a clean disposable restart, check
    the chest, its item and the Remote binding again.
