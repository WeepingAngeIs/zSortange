Anya Storage Plus moonconsole.1 visual playtest

In a disposable world, bind the Cupid Tablet by crouch-using it on your hub.
With the tablet selected, turn, walk and use it in the air. Try both main-hand
preferences and your usual skin. Escape during Cupid's blink, then reopen
and close the settled storage. Check grip contact at the outside bezel and
readability of the search field, first and last rows. Pause and resume while
holding the tablet. For repeated appearances, an operator can use
/anya storage settings set expansion.tabletOpeningChancePercent 100
on the disposable server and restore its previous value afterward.

Place the starter chest, UwU Storage Cube and hub side by side in the
disposable world. Open and close the UwU Storage Cube while another player
watches the split latch and listens. Open it with both players and close one
menu at a time. Move around the hinge and the hub's glass to check for flicker.
Compare your usual shaders and GUI scale. The Home Void Cube is the separate
entrance, and an operator can run /anya pocket status to see its setting.
