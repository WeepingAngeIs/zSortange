The isolated screen decoder bundles JCodec 0.2.5 for MP4 demultiplexing
and H.264 video (JCodec-LICENSE.txt), and JAAD 0.9.4 for AAC audio.

JAAD originally by in-somnia, with changes distributed by Tianscar.
